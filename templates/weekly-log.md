# Weekly Activity Log

A running record of what got worked on, week by week, for recall and as the raw material `resume-fodder.md` gets distilled from. Log what happened as it happens; periodically skim it to decide what is resume-worthy and promote that up.

**Scope reminder:** generalize away client and internal names. Describe the kind of work and its scale, not the specifics. No credentials or confidential figures.

## How to log

At the end of a session, or when a distinct chunk of work wraps, add or extend the entry for the current week: one short bullet per notable thread of work. Don't log routine or no-op sessions. A week with nothing worth noting gets no entry.

## Entries

### Week of YYYY-MM-DD

- One-line generalized description of a thread of work, and roughly how substantial it was (kicked off, continuing, wrapped up).
