Durable accomplishments worth resume language, distilled from [[weekly-log]] when something is significant, not every task. Review it whenever your resume is due an update, or at the end of an engagement.

**Scope reminder:** generalize away client and internal names. Describe scale, technology and outcome, the kind of thing that reads the same at any company.
